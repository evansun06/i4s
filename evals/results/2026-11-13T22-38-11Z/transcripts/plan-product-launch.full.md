## Final recommendation

Recommendation: launch a lean founding paid offer now, but make it sustainable.

Use **“Founding Nurse Rate: $8/month, locked while subscribed”** with a 10–14 day deadline. Promise only: **one practical nurse-specific money move per week** around shifts, overtime, debt, benefits, taxes, and burnout. Do **not** sell coaching, constant access, community, or live calls.

Because you have **1,200 free subscribers**, some stated willingness to pay, and only **10 hours/week**, the goal is validation—not building a big product. Compliments do not count; checkout does.

Watch these two numbers weekly:

1. **Net paid subscribers** = new paid minus cancellations/refunds.  
2. **Action signal rate** = percent of paid readers who reply, click, submit a question, or use/download the worksheet.

Pivot rules:

- If you get **fewer than 25 paid subscribers after the founding launch**, stop adding benefits. Interview 5–8 non-buyers and rewrite the positioning.
- If **net paid is flat or negative for 3 weeks**, change the promise/topic before changing the price.
- If action signal is under roughly **10–15% for 2–3 issues**, the content is probably too generic.
- If you consistently exceed **10 hours/week**, cut features immediately.

The strongest objection was that a cohort, Q&A, or community could make the launch feel more valuable. We reject that for now because your time cap and nursing schedule make access-based promises risky.

Next steps: build the checkout page, write one sample paid issue, send 3–5 launch emails over 14 days, publish weekly, batch replies twice weekly, and create at most one simple worksheet per month from your strongest issue.
