## Final recommendation

Recommendation: say yes only to a clearly documented acting EM rotation, not to an open-ended permanent move.

Your director is offering a real opportunity, and you are curious enough that it is worth testing. But your facts make this risky unless structured well: you like coding, the company’s “you can switch back” promise has never been tested, two teammates have longer tenure, and one wanted the EM role. The biggest risks are not just whether you can manage; they are rollback credibility, team legitimacy, and resentment if the transition is mishandled.
