## Final recommendation

Treat this as a firm roommate-boundary conversation, not a casual hint or a rent-only issue.

You’re on a joint lease for 7 more months, you otherwise like your roommate, and the partner is staying 4–5 nights a week. That is frequent enough to affect privacy, shared space, and utilities, even if your roommate calls it “barely here.” Your goal should be a workable agreement through lease-end, not escalation unless necessary.

Say something like:

> “Can we talk tonight for 15 minutes? I like living with you and want the next 7 months to work. But when your partner stays over 4–5 nights a week, it doesn’t feel like an occasional guest—it changes privacy, shared space, and utilities. When I brought it up before, it felt brushed off, so I need us to make an actual agreement.  
>   
> My preference is two planned overnight nights per week, with extra nights discussed in advance. If it becomes more than that regularly, then we need to talk about a fair utility/rent contribution and whether that actually works for the apartment.”

If they deflect, say:

> “I’m not asking you to defend your relationship. I’m saying this living arrangement isn’t working for me, and I’m not comfortable leaving it undefined.”

The main caution: don’t rush to the landlord. Because you’re both on the lease, a guest violation could create problems for both of you. Also, don’t make money the only solution if your real issue is crowding/privacy.

Next steps: check the lease guest policy, propose the two-night rule, put any agreement in writing, and if they refuse, send a written recap of your boundary and start tracking overnights objectively.
